New Laws Target Gay Community: Death Penalty for ‘Habitual Sodomy’

April 30, 2026

Amnesty International has released a critical analysis of the January 2026 Criminal Procedure Regulation, highlighting draconian provisions that institutionalize violence against marginalized groups. The regulation formalizes a system where human rights are secondary to discretionary religious interpretation.

Death Penalty: The most severe provision includes the prescription of the death penalty for "habitual sodomy." This sentence can be handed down based on a discretionary judgment from an Imam if they deem it is in the "public interest," removing standard evidentiary requirements and fair trial protections.

Torture and Ill-treatment: The regulation explicitly prescribes flogging or lashing for a wide range of "moral offences." These punishments are often carried out in public settings, intended to serve as a deterrent while violating international prohibitions on torture.

Social Hierarchy: Under the new rules, offenders are divided into four distinct categories. This created a sliding scale of punishment based on social status, ensuring that those in lower social tiers face the most brutal iterations of the law.

Religious Erasure: The law mandates severe penalties for any deviation from the Hanafi School of Sunni Islam. Beliefs held by religious minorities or interpreted as "contrary" to this specific branch are officially designated as heretical.
