Our Mission

Our sole purpose in life is to present you with easily readable and important LGBT news and emergency health, legal and safety contacts for all of our community, whether they are local, expat or traveling through. With weekly news videos also coming soon, you'll always have access to the latest LGBT news.

As begin this journey, we have initially used AI generated content to populate the site, but we are moving through the countries one by one and giving each country the attention it deserves with the most up-to-date news and information that you deserve. It will be worth it. All our human pages are marked GWN so you know who or what is providing the news. Please watch our progress bar and support our researchers and writers, and why not become one too? Feel free to contact us and help to make the community more aware and safer in a troubled world.

Although we do provide travel information, we do not pretend to be expert travel guides. We provide a hub-type experience to provide our shared experience as well as the best links to fabulous travel guides such as the Nomadic Boys who provide a fun and professional user experience for where to go and what to do in many countries.

Privacy & Safety

Your Responsibility: While we do our absolute best to verify all information is correct at time of writing, things change quickly and mistakes can be made. As with any media organization, we cannot take responsibility for your actions based on a possible error we have made during our research.

Your Data: We do not track your personal identity. Our search tools and maps are designed for anonymous use.

Contacting Us: If you use our contact form, we only use your email to reply to your inquiry. We never sell your information to third parties or advertisers.

Cookies: We use minimal essential cookies (like Google Translate) to ensure the site functions correctly in your language. By using GWN, you agree to this essential functionality.
